Support & connection help

Get your game, phone and dashboard on the same page.

Email supportDownload Connect

Contact

Email etadeove@gmail.com. Include your TADEX and Connect versions, iPhone/iPad model, operating-system version, game and platform, the step that failed and the exact error. Screenshots are helpful if you remove personal information. Never send passwords, card details, purchase receipts containing private information or a session access link.

No live data

Start a live session in TADEX; demo does not listen to game telemetry. Put the phone and game device on the same local network, allow local network access on iOS, use the displayed phone IP and UDP port, and start driving. Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation may block traffic. Keep the app active and the device unlocked.

Games that use Connect

Download the Windows companion below and use the game’s guide in Connection. Close a truck or rally game before Prepare game. In Connect paste the phone’s TADEX Connect link, then Start connection. Packets sent by the PC do not by themselves prove that the phone received them; check the phone’s received/valid counters.

Restore PRO

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy PRO and an Internet connection. The website and Windows companion cannot activate an App Store license. If the store reports a pending purchase, wait for its confirmation.

Data fields and full screen

TADEX shows only the fields received from the game. Select gear, speed and RPM and their sizes in Customize. The RPM bar remains in full screen. Tap the screen or press Escape to leave full screen. A shift cue requires an actual engine maximum or your configured reference; not every game supplies one.
