YOUR TELEMETRY. YOUR NETWORK.

Your game. Your cockpit.

Live racing telemetry on iPhone and iPad. Your phone receives the data, builds your dashboard and shares it across your local network.

Download Connect

Built for your driving

Choose digital instruments, nine analog dial designs or a focused gear display with a configurable shift cue. Adjust speed, gear, RPM, sizes and colors. Portrait and landscape layouts use the available screen. Full screen keeps the readings and RPM bar visible.

Keep it local

No TADEX account, advertising or analytics SDK. Driving data stays on your local network. Demo is clearly marked as simulated data. A live session needs a supported game and its telemetry output.

One companion for Windows

TADEX Connect brings the tools for Euro Truck Simulator 2, American Truck Simulator and other games into one installer. No terminal commands, Python or Node installation.

Start with Free

Free includes Forza Horizon 5, two live dashboards, one external browser and the full demo. PRO adds all implemented games, all dashboards and customization, with up to eight external browsers depending on your device and network.
