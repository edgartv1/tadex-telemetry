Privacy policy

How TADEX handles app, companion, website and support information.

Download Connect

Responsible developer

Edgar Tadeo Vergara Velasco is responsible for TADEX Telemetry and TADEX Connect. Privacy and support contact: etadeove@gmail.com.

App and local telemetry

TADEX does not require an account and does not send your driving telemetry to the developer. It receives game telemetry over your local network, processes current readings in memory, and displays the fields that the game actually supplies. Settings, language, dashboard preferences and verified purchase access are stored on your device. No advertising, behavioral tracking, cloud telemetry archive, location, contacts, microphone or camera collection is implemented.

Local sharing

If you enable browser sharing, your phone hosts a local HTTP/WebSocket dashboard. Browsers with the session link can see its telemetry. Browser preferences are stored locally. Session links contain an access token and expire when the session stops. Local HTTP, WebSocket and game UDP are not encrypted. Share only on trusted networks; do not publish a session link or expose the service to the Internet. Connect sends numeric telemetry to the private IPv4 destination you select.

Windows companion

Connect reads supported game telemetry interfaces, game install locations and selected configuration files. It stores local preferences and configuration backups so that its own changes can be restored. Game telemetry is not uploaded to TADEX. The iRacing reader excludes driver-name and session-document fields from its outgoing packet. GT7 console packets are decoded on the PC before local forwarding. A Microsoft runtime download, if requested, contacts Microsoft and is subject to Microsoft’s privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes iOS purchases and restores. TADEX uses StoreKit transaction verification and stores purchase access locally; it does not receive or store your card details. Apple’s payment, fraud-prevention and App Store practices are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Optional sharing of Apple diagnostics is controlled through your Apple device settings.

Website and downloads

These pages and the Connect download are hosted on GitHub Pages. TADEX adds no analytics, tracking cookies, external fonts or contact forms. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security and processes requests under its own privacy statement. Opening third-party links or emailing support uses the provider you choose and its own privacy terms.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot and meet applicable legal obligations, not for advertising or sale. Do not send passwords, full payment details, private session links or other people’s personal information. Email providers may process messages outside your country. We retain support correspondence only as necessary for those purposes and applicable legal duties; deletion requests are assessed against those duties.

Your choices and rights

Stop a session to stop reception and sharing. Remove the app to erase its local data, subject to device backup settings; uninstalling does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase. Connect settings and backups can be removed from its user data folder after restoring any game changes you want to reverse. To request access, correction, deletion/cancellation, objection, restriction of use or withdrawal of consent for support data, email etadeove@gmail.com with “Privacy request”, identify the request and an address for the reply. We may request proportionate proof of identity before disclosing data and respond within applicable legal periods. You may also contact your competent privacy authority.

Children and changes

TADEX does not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us to request removal of support information supplied by a child. Updates to this notice are published at this URL with an effective date. New optional uses of personal information require the notice and any consent required by law before they begin.
